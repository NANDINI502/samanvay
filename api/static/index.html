MedBot Orchestrator

connecting… Clinical decision support designed for health workers and doctors in Indian primary health centres. Grounded in the WHO PEN protocol for primary health care in low-resource settings. Runs on Nebius Token Factory + NVIDIA Nemotron.
⚠ Decision support, not diagnosis. Answers come only from cited WHO/NIH excerpts and published equations, and the system defers to a clinician on red flags, referral criteria or weak evidence. It does not replace clinical judgment. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Hackathon prototype — not a medical device, and not deployed in any health centre. Core protocol: WHO PEN — Package of Essential Noncommunicable Disease Interventions for Primary Health Care in Low-Resource Settings. Guideline answers are grounded in cited WHO/NIH excerpts. eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021) and FIB-4 are published equations; the cardiac model is trained on a small 1988 UCI research dataset (see DATASETS.md).
